# activity-stream adoption

<!-- Generated by php artisan standards:install from tey/standards; do not edit. -->

Publish activity through the activity-stream service's verb guide and authoriser, and render its feed; never a hand-built activity table. The feed is a side effect, never the source (Jasper, Nov 22) — a domain event drives the business work and the feed is one of its subscribers, so no business listener (billing, access, agreements, notification routing) listens to the activity-stream service's own events (they fire only when feed rows change), and an Action dispatches a domain event implementing PublishesToFeed instead of publishing inline; only a listener that shapes the feed itself, marked @feed-presentation with its reason.
