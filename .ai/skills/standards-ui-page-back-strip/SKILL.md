# Breakout page back strip

<!-- Generated by php artisan standards:install from tey/standards; do not edit. -->

When to break out: a page breaks out when it replaces its parent's shared layout instead of rendering inside it. A page drawn inside the parent's layout (one of its tabs, a settings section, a panel of the record) keeps its trail and does not break out. A page that leaves it (a module opened from a card-grid section landing, a bench, an editor, a full-screen record) declares breaksOut() or the kit.breakout route default. The strip skips the module's own crumbs: on a module's tab page (/billing, /billing/invoices) it goes to the landing above the module (Back to Admin), and a record under a tab goes back to its tab (tey/kit 0.45.0+). The strip is the kit's banded strip, never a plain link. Where the section also keeps its breadcrumbs, kit.breakout => 'with-trail' (tey/kit 0.45.0+) draws the whole trail on top and the banded strip below it: the strip goes back one level, and the full trail lets a deeply nested page jump straight to any ancestor. Which levels stay in the layout (tabs, pills, sidebar, hub) is ui-page.page-tabs. No hand-written back links.

Scaffold: `php artisan standards:make page --flavor=show <Name>`.

Kit: tey/kit PageViewModel::breaksOut(), tey/kit route default kit.breakout.
